# mujoco_rig

A shooter rig, welded to the 2026 field near the blue HUB, whose own nodes
shoot its eight preloaded FUEL into the HUB under the
[MuJoCo gateway](../../talOS/simulation/mujoco/README.md#the-mujoco-gateway),
with the score counted by the field's
[game rules](../../talOS/simulation/mujoco/fields/rebuilt_2026/README.md#rules).
It was made the way a student makes a robot:
`bazel run //tools:new_robot -- mujoco_rig --mechanism flywheel --lang cpp --dir examples`,
then `bazel run //tools:new_subsystem -- feeder --lang cpp --type velocity --robot examples/mujoco_rig`.
The two nodes are those tools' velocity nodes, unchanged: each drives its
motor at the velocity its `talos::Target` asks for, and nothing else. The
framework's `teleop` node turns two joystick buttons into their targets.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `main_processor/configuration/robot.toml` | the robot: tick, timeouts, target policy (`[robot.targets]`, teleop only), `[[subsystems]]` |
| `main_processor/configuration/robot_sim.toml` | the simulation-only config: includes `robot.toml` and adds `sim_gateway`, the MuJoCo gateway, its world (`[simulation.mujoco]`: the model, the field `rebuilt_2026`, seed 1), the teleop bindings, the simulated motor gains and the beam breaks' model sensors |
| `main_processor/configuration/subsystems/*.toml` | `flywheel` and `feeder` (each a motor and a beam break; ids and channels are placeholders), `teleop`, `telemetry` |
| `main_processor/configuration/mujoco/robot_params.toml` | the rig's [generator](../../talOS/simulation/mujoco/robots/README.md) parameters; `robot.xml` beside it is generated from them, and `robot_model_sync_test` fails, printing the command, when it is stale |
| `main_processor/configuration/*_manifest.bzl` | generated by `//tools:sync_manifest` from each config |
| `main_processor/{flywheel,feeder}/` | the nodes, their messages and their node tests |
| `BUILD` | `:sim` (`talos_sim`), `:sim_test`, `:shot_test`, `:model_twin_test` |

## Run it

Button 3 spins the flywheel to 41 rps, button 4 runs the feeder at 30 rps.
The rig's place on the field is the gateway's `--start-pose`: `:sim` passes
it in its `args`, and `./sim.sh` takes it as a gateway argument, because no
config key holds it yet (TODO.md SIM-18). Disabled for 1 s, then TELEOP,
the sticks at rest first so `teleop` arms, the flywheel from 2 s, the
feeder from 4 s:

```sh
bazel run //examples/mujoco_rig:sim -- --duration-s 10 \
  --sim-gateway-arg --mode-schedule --sim-gateway-arg disabled:1,teleop:9 \
  --sim-gateway-arg --joystick-schedule \
  --sim-gateway-arg '0:axes=0;buttons=0;pov=-1|2:axes=0;buttons=4;pov=-1|4:axes=0;buttons=12;pov=-1'

./sim.sh --config examples/mujoco_rig/main_processor/configuration/robot_sim.toml \
  --sim-gateway --no-studio --duration-s 10 \
  --gateway-arg --start-pose --gateway-arg 1.907,5.302,2.705 \
  --mode-schedule disabled:1,teleop:9 \
  --joystick-schedule '0:axes=0;buttons=0;pov=-1|2:axes=0;buttons=4;pov=-1|4:axes=0;buttons=12;pov=-1'
```

Both use the config's UDP ports, 5802 and 5803, so a second session on the
same host (another simulation, the RoboRIO simulation) collides with them;
`./sim.sh` has no flag for them.
`:sim` passes launcher flags through, and the launcher gives its ports to
the gateway: add `--remote 127.0.0.1 --remote-port 5812 --local-port 5813`
after `--` (or any two free ports).

The gateway's last line (in `sim/launcher.log` under `./sim.sh`) is the
match, for example `mujoco_gateway: stepped_s=9.920 dropped_s=0.015
dropped_ticks=3 overspeed_excursions=0 period=Teleop blue_points=8
red_points=0 shots=8`. Without `--no-studio`, Studio shows the match as the
`sim/state/match/*` channels (`/sim/state/match`: points, scored and shot
counts, period, stage) and the nodes' `flywheel/state/*` and
`feeder/state/*`. To see the field in Studio's 3D viewport:

```sh
bazel build //talOS/simulation/mujoco/fields/rebuilt_2026:rebuilt_2026_glb
```

then choose "Load field GLB" in the 3D Viewport panel and pick
`bazel-bin/talOS/simulation/mujoco/fields/rebuilt_2026/rebuilt_2026.glb`.

## The rig

This rig is a test fixture for the chain (nodes, launcher, bridge, MuJoCo
gateway, game rules), not a robot to copy dimensions from. Its 1.6 m frame,
single-file chute and 2.0 kg, 0.01 kg·m² flywheel are chosen to work around
the scripted shooter launching pieces too close together (TODO.md SIM-17):
a 1.6 m frame is not a legal FRC frame, and 0.01 kg·m² is more than a pair
of 4 in wheels can have (about 0.001 to 0.005 kg·m²). From the generator's
`shooter_rig` example, with what the shot needed changed:

- **A single-file chute.** The hopper is 0.17 m wide, one FUEL, and the frame
  1.6 m long, so the eight preloads lie in one row on its floor in front of
  the feeder. `shooter_rig`'s pocket holds three FUEL abreast; the scripted
  shooter launches the next as soon as the last is its two radii from the
  exit, so they leave touching, the second catches the first (gravity has
  slowed it along the launch axis) and their spin throws both off their
  paths: in the 30 perturbed runs below, `shooter_rig` at 42 rps scored 2 of
  8 in 28 and 3 in 2. Stacked in a narrow hopper (two columns of four, 0.17
  to 0.24 m wide), the FUEL arch over the feeder and jam: 5 to 25 % of
  perturbed runs launched only 2 to 5.
- **A heavier flywheel**, 2.0 kg and 0.01 kg·m² (`shooter_rig`: 0.8 kg,
  0.0025 kg·m²): a launch takes about 0.8 rps from it instead of 3. It
  coasts in neutral (`brake = false`).
- **Beam breaks**, `feeder_beam` across the chute where a FUEL waits for the
  feeder, `shooter_beam` under the feeder's roller (a beam across the pocket
  would read nothing: the flywheel takes a FUEL in the step it enters).
  Each reads false while closed (`sim_closed_reads` is false).
- Motor and beam names are the subsystem files'.

## The shot

The start pose `1.907,5.302,2.705` puts the rig 3.0 m from the blue HUB's
scoring site, 25° toward the DEPOT side of straight on, its rear (the
shooter's side) toward the HUB, clear of the TOWER and DEPOT. Measured with
the rig's model assembled as the gateway assembles it, no processes, flywheel
in velocity mode with the config's gains, feeder at 3.6 V (30 rps × 0.12 V
per rps):

| Flywheel (rps) | 37 | 38 | 39 | 40 | 41 | 42 | 43 | 44 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scored of 8 | 0 | 2 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 0 |

41 rps is the middle of the window. 30 runs with the pose moved up to 2 cm
in x and y and 0.01 rad in yaw, the speed up to 2 % either way, the seed
drawn and each tick up to 200 µs off its 5 ms scored 240 of 240, and so did
the same runs with the feeder at 2.5 V and at 6 V. Seed 1 draws red
inactive first at TELEOP's tie, so the blue HUB counts through SHIFT 1 as
well as TRANSITION.

## Tests

`shot_test` (e2e) runs the launcher on `robot_sim.toml` with the schedules
above and judges the session's record (`/sim/state/match` in telemetry's
event log, `/hw/state` in the bridge's): all 8 preloads launched and at
least 7 scored in TELEOP (every measured session scored 8; one of margin), each beam break closed and open, every member exiting 0,
the graph accepted without `--allow-graph-errors`. It ends the session on
the record, not on a wall-clock time: once all eight have scored, or the
schedule clock is 3 s past the eighth launch or 30 s past its start.
`//talOS/simulation/mujoco/robots:rig_shot_test` is the same scene without
processes, at least 7 of 8 and the same `/sim/state/match` every run, on
talOS's copy of the model (talOS does not depend on an example);
`model_twin_test` holds the two models to the same bytes. `sim_test`
(`talos_sim_test`) starts, runs and stops the rig cleanly. The nodes keep the
node test matrix `new_subsystem` gave them.
